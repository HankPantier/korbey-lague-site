## Built for the Industries We Know Best

We don't try to be everything to everyone. The industries below represent genuine depth — teams who understand how these businesses are structured, how they get paid, where the tax exposure lives, and what the growth inflection points look like.

### Healthcare Professionals
icon: Stethoscope

Practice owners, dentists, therapists, and specialists face billing complexity, compensation structuring, and regulatory compliance that generic accounting can't handle well. We work with healthcare professionals on entity structure, retirement planning, and the financial side of running a practice — not just the tax return.

### Contractors and Trades
icon: Hammer

Job costing, equipment financing, bonding requirements, subcontractor payments — the financial layer of a contracting business is its own discipline. Whether you run a crew of five or a multi-project operation, we keep your books clean and your cash flow visible.

### Nonprofits
icon: HeartPulse

Grant compliance, Form 990 preparation, board reporting, and fund accounting require a different framework than for-profit bookkeeping. Korbey Lague PLLP brings that framework to nonprofits operating in Massachusetts and beyond.

### Service-Based Businesses
icon: Briefcase

Consultants, agencies, professional services firms — your revenue is often tied to your own calendar, which makes cash flow planning and tax timing critical. We help service businesses build financial systems that scale without adding administrative chaos.

### Business Startups
icon: Lightbulb

Getting the structure right at the beginning saves real money later. From entity selection and initial bookkeeping setup to projections for a first funding conversation, we work with founders who want to start on solid ground.

## What Working With Us Actually Looks Like

New clients often tell us they assumed working with a CPA firm would be complicated. It doesn't have to be. Here's what the experience actually looks like from first contact to ongoing work.

### We Start With a Real Conversation
Before any paperwork, we sit down — virtually or in person — and talk about where your business is and where you're trying to take it. This isn't a sales call. It's how we figure out whether we're the right fit and what you actually need.

### We Assess and Onboard
Once we're aligned, we review your current financial setup, identify gaps, and get your books and systems into shape. For clients using Sage Intacct, we handle the configuration. For others, we'll recommend the right tools for your size and complexity.

### We Deliver Ongoing Advisory Work
This is where most firms stop showing up — and where we don't. Depending on your service tier, you'll have regular check-ins, proactive tax planning touchpoints, and direct access to your advisor when something comes up. Not a helpdesk ticket. A conversation.

### We Grow With You
As your business changes, your financial needs change too. Acquisitions, new locations, new service lines — we're already in the picture, so the advisory work stays current.
